Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

FastVPN ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled when you use the FastVPN Android application.

What we collect

To provide a working VPN connection, the app sends the following to our servers when you connect:

  • A device encryption key (WireGuard public key) — generated on your device, used to set up your encrypted tunnel. This key cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • An assigned internal VPN IP address — a private address (e.g. 10.8.0.x) used only for routing your traffic through the tunnel.
  • Basic app diagnostics — such as app version and general connection status, used to keep the service reliable.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not log or store your browsing activity, the websites or services you connect to, or timestamps of your online activity while connected.
  • We do not require an account, email address, or any personally identifying information to use the app.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.

Advertising

FastVPN is free to use and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other technical information to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google settings at any time.

Permissions

The app requests the Android VPN permission (to create the encrypted tunnel) and the notification permission (to show connection status). Neither is used to access or read your other app data.

Data retention

Your device's registration (encryption key and assigned address) is kept only for as long as needed to maintain your connection, and is removed when you disconnect or after a period of inactivity.

Children's privacy

FastVPN is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
